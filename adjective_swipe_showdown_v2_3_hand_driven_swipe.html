How to Play

This is a swipe left or swipe right game.
1. Choose a question number.
2. Read the sentence.
3. Tap the adjective.
4. Correct answer unlocks the swipe card.
5. Swipe LEFT to keep the surprise.
6. Swipe RIGHT to give the surprise to another team.
Steal Rule: If a team taps the wrong word, the next team gets one steal chance. If the steal is wrong, the game moves to the next question.

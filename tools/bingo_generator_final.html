How To Use

  1. Click Create Now.
  2. Type or paste one vocabulary word or phrase per line.
  3. Choose 3×3 or 5×5 and set how many cards to create.
  4. Click Display Bingo Card to preview one shuffled card.
  5. Use Pick a Bingo Word as your random classroom caller.
  6. Click Create Printable Cards, then Print / Save as PDF.
  7. Click Download Word Cards if you want editable bingo cards in Word.

How To Use

  1. Click Create Now to open the crossword generator.
  2. Enter a puzzle title, level, difficulty, and instructions.
  3. Type one word and clue per line, for example: sunny - The weather is bright and hot.
  4. Click Create Puzzle to preview the worksheet.
  5. Use Print / Save PDF, Download Word, or Word Puzzle + Key.

Tip: Use 5–20 words for the best crossword shape.

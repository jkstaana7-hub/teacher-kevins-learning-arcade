How to Play

1. Teacher chooses 2–6 teams and team avatars.

2. The current team discusses and clicks an answer.

3. Correct answer gives points automatically.

4. Wrong answer opens a Steal popup.

5. One steal attempt only: correct steal = +50 points, wrong steal = -50 points.

6. The Power Wheel affects scores: Double Points, Triple Points, Bonus Question, Lucky +50, Shield, Steal 50, Give 50, Swap Scores, Lose 50, or Team Choice.
